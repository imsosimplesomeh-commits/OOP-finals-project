Follow the displayed order, postponing ST-07 and CL-05 until after EN-01, TE-05 and the dependent-subject part of SU-05 until after CL-01, and GC-03 and the protected-enrollment part of EN-06 until after SC-01. Complete the report setup listed in RP-03 just before Part B4 (RP-01 to RP-04), as directed by the source.
